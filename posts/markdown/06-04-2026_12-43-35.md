## Intro

Hello, my name is Bradley Hutchings. I am a recent graduate of the
[Colorado School of Mines](https://www.mines.edu/) with a B.S in Computer Science.
I recently have found a fondness of cabinetry and wood working since taking
a job as a cabinet maker.

## My qualifications are as follows:

### Diego Periasco Woodworks:

I worked for a month at Diego's workshop. There I learned how to become proficient
in reading measurement devices, making repeatable cuts, calibrating and maintaining
machinery such as a small drum sander and saw stop table saw. I mostly focused on
building cabinets and was let go before time came to install them into the clients
homes.

### TaskRabbit:

I worked for a month and a half as an on call handyman. I would install TV
systems, build furniture, install smart home devices, and other miscellaneous
work. I mostly worked in SF and would work directly with clients to achieve their
goals, whatever that may have been.

### Fusion 360 Certified:

During my time in highschool, I took a class at the Napa Valley College to get
certified in a [C.A.D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer-aided_design) software
by the name of [Fusion 360](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autodesk_Fusion). I learned
how to run 3D Printers which then let me branch out into learning how to operate
CNC routers.

### FIRST Robotics:

Also during my time in highschool, I worked in a machine shop for a robotics team.
I worked a manual lathe and manual milling machine. I worked in Fusion 360 to
create draft diagrams of parts which then would be machined and implemented on the
competition robot. (Feel free to ask me more about this, I can talk about it for a while)

Thank you for reviewing a bit about me. You can find more about my background in
Computer Science on my [Website Homepage](https://breadleaf.github.io/).
